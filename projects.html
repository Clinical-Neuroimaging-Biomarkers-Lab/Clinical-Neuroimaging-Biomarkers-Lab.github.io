Current Projects

Our ongoing research initiatives

Australian Trauma Biobank: Understanding CPTSD and PTSD

Establishing a comprehensive biobank using multimodal approaches, MRI brain imaging, blood-based markers, physiological monitoring, and psychosocial assessments, to identify distinct neurobiological profiles associated with trauma-related disorders.

Learn more →

Brain Disconnection and Psychiatric Outcomes After Stroke

Investigating how structural and functional brain disconnections following stroke contribute to psychiatric complications, using advanced disconnectome mapping and large-scale stroke datasets to improve prediction of post-stroke outcomes.

Learn more →

Prevalence and Risk Factors of CPTSD in Australia

Investigating the prevalence of CPTSD in the Australian adult population, its association with childhood maltreatment and comorbid conditions, and identifying targets for prevention and intervention.

Learn more →

Trauma Reactions in Internally Displaced People in Ethiopia

Investigating the prevalence and impact of PTSD and CPTSD among internally displaced people in conflict-affected regions of Northwest Ethiopia, using culturally adapted clinical assessments.

Learn more →

Global Neuroimaging of CPTSD: An ENIGMA–Trauma Biobank Collaboration

The first large-scale international effort to investigate the neurobiology of CPTSD, integrating data from the ENIGMA-PTSD consortium with the Australian Trauma Biobank across thousands of trauma-exposed individuals worldwide.

Learn more →

Depression, Resilience, and Mental Health in the UK Biobank

Leveraging multimodal data from over 500,000 UK Biobank participants to investigate how brain biology, environmental adversity, and genetic risk interact to shape vulnerability and resilience to mental illness.

Learn more →
